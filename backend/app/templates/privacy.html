What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and, once you provide it, the country or city you are job-hunting in.
  • Your password — stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store it in a readable form and cannot recover it.
  • Skills extracted from your CV — the skill names we recognise, such as "Python" or "Project Management".
  • Your activity in the product — your chosen target role, your progress against your roadmap, and your conversations with the career advisor.

Your CV file

We do not store your CV. When you upload a PDF or DOCX, it is held in memory only for as long as it takes to read the text and match it against our list of known skills. The file is never written to disk and is discarded as soon as that finishes. Only the extracted skill names are saved.

How your data is processed

Skill extraction, gap analysis, roadmaps and the career advisor all run on our own servers using a fixed rule-based engine and a built-in knowledge base. Your CV and your messages are not sent to any third-party AI service, and they are not used to train any model.
